HOW IT WORKS

From search to review.

01

Find a job

Search by title, keyword, state, category, or employment type.

02

Review details

Read the responsibilities, requirements, employer, location, and compensation information provided.

03

Submit application

Complete the application form. A résumé is not requested at this first stage.

04

Wait for review

Your application ends at Waiting for Review. If there is a next step, you will be contacted through email.

What we may ask for

  • Name and contact information
  • City and state
  • Work experience and recent job title
  • Relevant certifications or licenses
  • Availability and professional summary

What not to submit

  • Social Security number
  • Bank account or card information
  • Passwords or security codes
  • Payment for an application
  • Unrelated sensitive personal information
