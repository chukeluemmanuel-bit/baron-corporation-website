About Baron Corporation

Engineering that stays close to the work.

We support facilities, infrastructure, equipment, building systems, and project teams with practical technical services built around real operating conditions.

Our multidisciplinary structure allows engineering, maintenance, fabrication, installation, troubleshooting, and project coordination to work together instead of operating in isolation.

Focused on safety, reliability, operational efficiency, and long-term facility performance.
