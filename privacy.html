Privacy & safety

Your information deserves clear handling.

This page explains what the career-interest application collects, why it is collected, and what you should never send through the website.

On this pageInformation collectedHow it is usedSafety guidanceContact
01

Information collected

The application may collect your job title of interest, preferred work state, name, email address, phone number, city/state, years of experience, recent job title, certifications, availability, best time to call, professional summary and additional information you choose to provide.

02

How information is used

Application information is used to review your career interest, understand your experience, and contact you if management identifies a suitable next step. Application data may be delivered by email and stored in the project database for management review.

03

What you should never submit

Do not send

Social Security numbers, banking/card details, passwords, verification codes or payment information.

No application fee

Baron Corporation does not charge applicants to submit the initial career-interest application on this website.

04

Questions or corrections

For questions about information you submitted, contact mgt.baroncorporation@gmail.com.
